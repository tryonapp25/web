FAQ

Does TryOn sell products?

No. TryOn is a preview-only tool to help you decide before buying from any store.

Will the preview be 100% accurate?

Previews are visual approximations. Results depend on image quality, pose, and lighting.

How does tokens work?

1 try-on = 1 token. Tokens are deducted only when a generation succeeds.

What photos work best?

Front-facing, good lighting, full body visible, and no heavy filters.
